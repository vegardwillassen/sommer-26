Good to know

Buses 101 / 102

Your car replacement. Every 15 to 20 minutes in summer between Ayia Napa, Cape Greco, Konnos and Protaras. €2 cash to the driver (€3 after 21:00) and strictly cash only: no cards, no phone pay, small notes. Doing three or more bus days? A day pass is €6, 5 days €20, a week €25 — but the driver sells only single tickets and day passes; buy the 5-day or weekly pass at an OSEA office or authorised kiosk (ask reception for the nearest). The OSEA stops are on Makariou Avenue 2 to 3 minutes up from the hotel; live times at osea.com.cy.

The harbour

A 10 minute downhill walk from Napa Plaza. All boat trips leave from here, and the kiosks on the quay often beat online prices if you book the evening before.

Sun hours

Sunrise about 05:50, sunset about 19:50 in late July, and the full moon rises on Wednesday 29 July. Midday is 33 to 35 degrees: beach, spa or museum then, cliffs and walks early or late.

Booking ahead

Late July is peak season. Book boat trips, Sage, Stamna and Vassos one or two days ahead, the Varosha tour and horse riding 2 to 3 days ahead. Everything else is walk-in.

Crossing north

Famagusta, Varosha and North Nicosia require your passport, or a Norwegian nasjonalt ID-kort med reiserett, at the Green Line; bring the passports anyway as the safest document at the checkpoints. Euros are accepted on the other side. Note that consular help north of the line is very limited, so keep your documents on you and safe.

Taxis

Short hops around town run €5 to €10, Nissi or the Marina €10 to €15. Agree the price before you get in, or ask the hotel to call one.

Street smarts

Bar Street PRs will offer you deals: never take the first one, the next bar beats it. Skip quad and buggy rentals that demand cash deposits, they are the classic holiday scam here. Prices at the quay kiosks usually beat Viator and other online platforms for the same boats.
